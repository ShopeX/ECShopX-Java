# Files

- [Aftersales Domain (ecshopx-aftersales)](aftersales.md) - The after-sales Bundle — apply (port-based header/trade resolution and per-unit split), review, refund-check, sendback, close and partial-cancel flows over the aftersales/aftersales_detail/aftersales_refund tables, the scheduled refund pipeline that fans out to per-channel refund executors, WXA member notify surfaces, and every dispatch event the domain publishes.
- [Goods Domain (ecshopx-goods)](goods.md) - The product Bundle — item/SKU/category/tag/barcode management, platform-vs-supplier goods flows, three-pool inventory orchestration (ItemInventoryOrchestratorService, InventoryDeductTargetResolver, Redis item_store keys), checkout-time goods services for order create, the operator-cart facade family including the shopadmin fast-buy cloud bucket and platform-store resolution (OnsalePlatformStoreSupport), the wxapp list orchestrator, and the OME / Jushuitan / WdtErp item and inventory sync surfaces.
- [Marketing and Loyalty Domains (promotions, kaquan, point, pointsmall, deposit, employee-purchase)](marketing.md) - The six bundles that run promotions, coupons, member grades, points, stored value and internal purchase — promotions owns the central WXA subscribe sender (WxaTemplateMsgActivityRemindSendService) that the order/member/aftersales notice ports funnel into plus the four merchant lifecycle SMS notice job handlers, kaquan runs the coupon add/edit/delete fan-out to youshu and DM card-template sync behind the seckill cross-promotion guard and the trade-finish consume-card listener, point/pointsmall keep the point ledger and point-mall catalog, deposit handles recharge callbacks with Redis balance counters, and employee-purchase enforces per-item limits plus passphrase participation with a Redis quota and compensating rollback.
- [Members Domain (ecshopx-members)](members.md) - The member/account bundle — H5 and wxapp login orchestration behind espier's LoginController, HS256 JWT issuing/verification and blacklist-backed token refresh, member account creation with the post-commit CreateMemberSuccess dispatch fan-out to CRM/Shuyun/Youshu/promotions/points/datacube, member update and mobile-change flows, wechat fans/tag sync, the goods-arrival subscription surface (缺货订阅) whose wxapp subscribe-message notices fire from the goods bundle's event:258 item-store update, overseas trust login (Apple/Google/Facebook/Line), and member-side SMS verification codes.
- [Orders Domain (ecshopx-orders)](orders.md) - The order/cart/logistics/settlement Bundle — the two-level Trade vs NormalOrders/NormalOrdersItems model with order_class variants, the wxapp create-and-pay pipeline, the admin 代客下单 deferred create-and-pay pipeline (Redis snapshot, materialize-after-pay, refund-on-failure, cloud-warehouse fast-buy branch), the trade-finish → order pay-success bridge with the shopadmin/shopscreen ziti auto-complete, auto-cancel/finish cron families, statements settlement, invoicing, and the cross-bundle cart ports (goods and promotions) behind the front CartController surface.
- [Payments and Channels](payments.md) - The payment stack — ecshopx-payment settings/notify facades plus the channel bundles hfpay, adapay, bspay, and chinaums-pay — covering the notify endpoints and signature verification, the order-side vs deposit-side port inversion, channel refund executors, merchant withdrawals, and the XXL-Job retry loops.
- [Platform Services and Supporting Bundles (espier, super-admin, theme, data-cube, shopmenu-border, tdkset, comments, wsugc, im, form, one-code, tb-items, kujiale, shopex-ai)](platform-services.md) - The long tail of shared and supporting Bundles — espier as the platform capability core (pluggable file storage drivers behind ecshopx.storage.driver, Excel import chunking onto the dispatch bus, address trees, upgrade agreements, and the JWT/auth MVC interceptor stack that every admin and H5 request passes through), plus one short map of super-admin, theme, data-cube, shopmenu-border, tdkset, comments, wsugc, im, form, one-code, tb-items, kujiale and shopex-ai.
- [Stores, Distributors, Merchants, and Salespersons](stores-and-merchants.md) - The store-side and B2B2C Bundles — companies/shops/operators and product models (ecshopx-companys), distributors as physical stores with create/update after-commit fan-out (ecshopx-distribution), promoter QR codes and first-level brokerage (ecshopx-popularize), merchant onboarding with FrontMerchantAuth JWT (ecshopx-merchant), WeCom salesperson tasks and commissions (ecshopx-salesperson), plus ecshopx-selfservice, ecshopx-reservation, ecshopx-community group-buy chief write-off, and the supplier_order split out of normal orders (ecshopx-supplier).
- [WeChat Ecosystem (ecshopx-wechat, ecshopx-work-wechat)](wechat.md) - The WeChat integration bundles — ecshopx-wechat covers the Open Platform callback at /wechatAuth/callback (subscribe/fans/tag + wx-shops audit events into the dispatch bus), the dual-mode authorizer token service, mini-program publish/audit orchestration with wxa-template fallbacks, the wxjava HTTP clients (qrcode, url link, jscode2session, live broadcast), official-account fans/tag services, and the legacy /wechatAuth/wxapp qrcode route; ecshopx-work-wechat covers WeCom callbacks, notice textcard/miniprogram messages, and the DIS work-wechat H5 base URIs.
